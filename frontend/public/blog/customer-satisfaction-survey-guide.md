## What Is a Customer Satisfaction Survey?

A customer satisfaction survey is a structured questionnaire designed to measure how happy customers are with your product, service, or overall experience.

It answers questions like:
- “How satisfied are our customers right now?”
- “What frustrates them?”
- “What delights them?”
- “Which improvements will make the biggest impact?”

The most common CSAT question is:

> **“How satisfied are you with your experience?”**  
> (Scale: 1 – Very dissatisfied → 5 – Very satisfied)

Simple. Fast. Powerful.

## When Should You Send a CSAT Survey?

The timing of your CSAT survey impacts response rates and accuracy.

### **1. Immediately after a support interaction**
Example:
- After a chat session  
- After a ticket is resolved  
- After a call with an agent  

This measures service quality.

### **2. After a product milestone**
Such as:
- Completing a purchase  
- Finishing onboarding  
- Using a new feature  

This helps determine product fit and usability.

### **3. Quarterly for overall brand sentiment**
A regularly scheduled CSAT helps you track trends and benchmark progress.

## How to Write High-Quality CSAT Questions

Customer satisfaction surveys must be short and focused. Stick to 3–7 questions.

Here are proven CSAT question formats:

### **1. Satisfaction rating (core question)**
**“How satisfied are you with your experience today?”**  
1–5 scale

### **2. Open-ended insight**
**“What could we improve?”**

This generates qualitative insights, which are often more valuable than ratings.

### **3. Feature-specific rating**
**“How satisfied are you with the ease of use of [Feature]?”**

### **4. Customer effort score (CES) question**
**“How easy was it to accomplish your goal?”**

This identifies friction points.

### **5. Likelihood to return or repurchase**
**“How likely are you to use us again?”**

Short, sharp, effective.

## Mistakes to Avoid When Writing a CSAT Survey

Even great companies make these errors:

### **✔ Asking too many questions**
Long surveys reduce response rates dramatically.

### **✔ Using biased or leading questions**
Avoid wording like:
- “How much do you love our product?”
- “Why do you find this feature useful?”

### **✔ Mixing multiple metrics**
CSAT ≠ NPS ≠ CES  
Keep these separate.

### **✔ Asking questions customers cannot answer**
If the customer has not used a feature, skip that question.

## Best Practices for Distributing CSAT Surveys

Distribution is just as important as the survey itself.

### **1. Meet customers where they already are**
Top channels:
- In-app surveys  
- Email  
- Post-purchase pages  
- Chat widgets  
- QR codes for in-person experiences  

### **2. Keep it lightweight**
One-click rating = higher completion rate.

### **3. Incentives increase response rates**
Examples:
- Discount coupons  
- Loyalty points  
- Small freebies  

### **4. Automate triggering**
Use event-based workflows such as:
- “Post-purchase completed”  
- “Support ticket closed”  
- “Subscription renewal”  

Automation ensures consistency.

## Turning CSAT Data Into Strategic Action

Here’s how companies turn survey results into growth:

### **1. Close the loop**
Respond personally to dissatisfied customers.

Even a short message helps:
> “We’ve reviewed your feedback and are fixing the issue.”

### **2. Share reports internally**
Teams that benefit:
- Product  
- Marketing  
- Support  
- Operations  

### **3. Prioritize improvements**
Use a simple system:
- High-impact + low-effort fixes first  
- High-impact + high-effort next  

### **4. Track before → after impact**
Measure whether changes actually increased satisfaction.
